Why this page exists

The clean isn’t the hard part

The hard part is the texts, emails, phone calls, access arrangements, reschedules, and tenant coordination that happen before we even arrive. At AirCare, we understand property management. That’s why we’ve built our service around making life easier for busy property managers.

Chasing tenants for access

Three texts, two voicemails and a reschedule. Per unit. Multiply that across a portfolio and chasing access becomes a job in itself. AirCare arranges the booking for you.

Prevent unnecessary service callouts

A neglected heat pump does not heat properly, and increases the risk of a breakdown. Professional maintenance extends a unit’s lifespan, and a replacement costs thousands.

Reduce tenant complaints

Get ahead of tenant complaints by ensuring their heat pump is running effectively. Winter is the busiest period for HVAC companies, and also when tenants realise they need a service.

One point of contact

If we find something that needs work we don’t do, we organise the trade that does. You still have only one point of contact.

For your rentals

The Healthy Homes Standard

The Healthy Homes heating standard isn’t just about having a heat pump on the wall. It’s about whether the main living area can be heated effectively to 18°C even on the coldest day of the year. A heat pump packed with dust doesn’t put out the heat it should, and a unit quietly underperforming is the sort of thing you hear about from the tenant first. Servicing buys time as well. A unit cleaned every year can last years longer, which puts off a replacement the owner has to fund and you have to explain.

Performing at its best

Dust and a blocked coil restrict airflow, so the unit works harder and delivers less heat. A proper clean gets it back to the best it can do.

Checked, not just cleaned

Every unit gets a condition check as part of the service. We’re looking for faults, weak performance and units that aren’t up to standard, not just dirt. If cleaning alone may not restore full performance, we’ll let you know.

Told, then sorted

If a unit isn’t performing as it should, you hear it from us in writing, with recommendations for further action. We can organise the trade to put it right, rather than leaving it on your desk.

We’re cleaners, not Healthy Homes assessors. We’ll tell you what we find and organise the fix, but the compliance sign-off stays with you.

Scope and pricing

What’s covered, and how it’s priced

We publish the pricing structure rather than a rate card, because the number depends on your portfolio. The structure doesn’t.

In scope

  • The Deep Clean as the base service on every heat pump: a thorough clean of both the inside and outside units.
  • A condition check on every unit. While we’re in there we look for faults, poor performance and units that aren’t up to standard. It all goes on the service record.
  • Units that need more than a clean. Heavy mould, a unit in poor condition, a fault we can put right on the day: we deal with it rather than handing it back to you.
  • Upgrades and add-ons per unit, as needed. Mould treatment and the like, recommended only where a unit actually calls for it. Never applied as a matter of course.
  • Fresh air and ventilation filter systems: HRV, DVS, SmartVent and others. If it moves air through the house, it’s in scope.
  • All tenant contact and access coordination, with a written service record on every unit the same day.

How pricing works

  • Fixed per unit

Being straight with you

What we don’t do — and what we do about it

We clean and service units, including the ones that need a good deal more than a standard clean. What we don’t do is refrigeration or electrical work. When we find a fault in that territory, we don’t just note it and leave it with you. We organise the trade and coordinate the tenant. You still have one point of contact.

Refrigeration work

Gas, regassing and mechanical repairs are a different trade and we don’t take them on. We arrange them for you through MJR Plumbing & Gas and the other Canterbury trades we work with. We handle the booking and the tenant.

Electrical work

Anything that needs a registered electrician goes to Insight Electrical. Same arrangement — we make the referral, book the job and organise access, so it never comes back to your desk as a new work order.

Common questions

What managers usually ask first

If yours isn’t here, call Ros. She can talk you through how a programme would work across your portfolio, and what it would cost.

Who chases the tenant for access?

We do, entirely. Once the work order is with us, we contact the tenant directly by text and call, book a time that suits them, and send a reminder text beforehand. You don’t hear from us again until the job is done. The one exception — a tenant who goes genuinely unreachable, and then we come back to you rather than let it sit.

What happens if the tenant isn’t home?

If a tenant misses the appointment, we reschedule and let you know so your work order isn’t left sitting open. We always send a reminder text to tenants on the day of the booking. A missed appointment fee may be billed for repeated missed appointments. The easiest way to avoid a missed visit is to provide us with a key or lockbox access. We do not require anyone to be home during the service.

How long from work order to job?

Typically within a week from September to March. Within two weeks from April to August, when everyone remembers their heat pump at once. Schedule the programme in the quiet season and you get both: the faster turnaround and the lower rate.

Do we have to use a new portal or system?

No. Most of our agencies email work orders through and manage them in Tapi, which we’re familiar with. If your office runs on something else, we’ll work to your process. Taking on a new supplier shouldn’t mean changing how your team works.

Do you invoice us or the owner?

Whichever you prefer, and we can split it by property if that suits your trust accounting. Per job or consolidated, through Tapi or by email. You tell us once and we set it up that way for the whole portfolio.

What if a unit needs a repair you don’t cover?

If the unit simply needs more than a standard clean, heavy mould or poor condition, we deal with it on the day. If it’s genuinely another trade, it goes on the service record and we organise it: refrigeration through MJR Plumbing & Gas, electrical through Insight Electrical. We make the referral, book the job and coordinate access. No fresh work order for you to raise.

Does a service make the property Healthy Homes compliant?

No, and be wary of anyone in our trade who tells you it does. We’re cleaners, not assessors: we don’t carry out Healthy Homes assessments and we can’t certify compliance. What a service does is make sure the heat pump is clean, working and putting out the best heat it can, which is the part of the heating standard that quietly slips as units age. An older or damaged unit may still fall short of what the room needs. If it does, you’ll have it in writing the same day, and we’ll organise the fix. The compliance call itself stays with you and your assessor.

Who decides on upgrades and add-ons?

You do, once, at the start. Most agencies have us apply recommended upgrades automatically and bill them with the job, because approving each one individually is exactly the admin they came to us to avoid. If you’d rather keep a closer eye on costs, we’ll quote and wait for your approval every time.

Can we trial you on a few properties first?

Yes, and we’d rather you did. There’s no minimum portfolio size and no contract to sign before you’ve seen how we handle a tenant. Send us a handful of units, see what comes back, and scale it up from there.

Are you insured, and what areas do you cover?

We carry public liability insurance and we’ve been trading for over 5 years. Three technicians, four vehicles. We cover Christchurch, Rangiora, Rolleston, Amberley and the surrounding areas.
