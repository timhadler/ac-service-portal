Why it matters

Your heat pump works hard. So does the mould inside it.

A dirty heat pump doesn’t just cost more to run — it blows allergens, bacteria and mould spores into the air your family breathes every day.

Lower power bills

Dust on coils forces the motor to work harder, burning more power and shortening the unit’s life.

Cleaner air for the whole whānau

Especially important for allergy and asthma sufferers — kids and kaumātua included.

Avoid costly breakdowns

Regular professional cleans prevent component failure and add years to your unit’s lifespan.

Eliminate musty odours

Only a professional deep clean can remove mould-caused smells from inside the unit.
