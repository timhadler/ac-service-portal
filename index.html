Why it matters

Why don't you just clean the filters?

Because the filter was never designed to protect your unit. It was designed to protect your air; catching lint, hair, and larger debris. Even spotless, a standard heat pump filter only reliably catches particles down to about 10 microns. Most household dust is finer than that. It sails right past the filter, and clogs up the thing that actually matters: the coils. Read more below.

Lower Power Bills

Over time, dust and debris build up in the coils, even with clean filters. This restricts airflow, forcing your heat pump to work harder, and increasing energy consumption. A clean system runs more efficiently and helps keep bills down.

Cleaner Air for the Whole Whānau

Especially important for allergy and asthma sufferers. Heat pumps can accumulate mould, bacteria, and dust over time. Without regular cleaning, these contaminants are circulated throughout your home.

Avoid Costly Breakdowns & Extend Lifespan

Regular professional cleaning clears blockages and reduces strain on key components, like the fan-motor. Repairing or replacing these components can be costly, getting your unit regularly cleaned can add years to its life.

Eliminate Musty Odours

A dirty or mouldy heat pump can produce musty, unpleasant smells. These odours won’t disappear on their own, only a thorough professional clean can fully remove them.
