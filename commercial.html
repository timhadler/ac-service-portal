What to do instead ↓

One or two high-wall units you can reach off a small ladder is a standard clean. Book it online at the standard rate and we’ll be there. No quote, no back-and-forth.

Ask for a quote instead if the units are high up, hard to get at, ducted or ceiling cassettes, or if we can only come outside your opening hours.

The bit you actually want to know

What this looks like in your space

We know organising trades around your business hours can be tricky. Here’s how we simplify the process.

About 45 minutes per unit

One unit at a time, so only one room is affected at once. You can tell us the running order on arrival and we will work to it.

Covered before we start

Drop sheets down, and the surfaces underneath and around the unit covered — bench, chair, basin, till, whatever is beneath it. Nothing gets worked on until it is protected.

What we need from you

We bring most of what we need. All we need from you is access to a power point close to the floor, and a water supply.

The unit, not the room

The clean is contained at the unit. Wash water is captured in a bag rather than run down your wall, which is the part people picture going wrong.

We leave it as we found it

Sheets up, surfaces wiped, filters back in, our rubbish out with us. The only sign we have been there will be a clean blowing heat pump.

Timing

When we can come

We work to your schedule. Tell us the window that costs you least and we will work inside it.

What we can offer

  • Saturdays. Already part of what we do. For a lot of clinics and salons this is the easiest answer.
  • Your closed day. Mondays for a lot of salons, Sundays or Mondays for hospitality. An empty building is the fastest job we do.
  • Around your bookings. Between patients, before service, in the gaps. Tell us the gaps and we will plan the visit into them.
  • Early hours by arrangement. Possible, and worth asking about. Talk to us and we will tell you straight whether we can do it.

What we will not do

  • Promise a start time we cannot keep. We would rather agree a window on the phone than publish one and miss it.
  • Turn up unannounced. The visit is agreed in advance and confirmed before we come.
  • Work through your busiest hour because it suits our run. If the only slot we have is a bad one for you, we will say so and find another.
Frequency

How often these actually need doing

Loading rate is what sets the interval, and it varies more between premises types than most people expect. A kitchen is not an office.

Premises Typical interval What drives it
Dental, GP, physio, chiro Annual Steady general loading, plus the perception problem of a visibly dirty vent above a patient.
Veterinary 6-monthly Hair and dander load the coil and the filter far faster than a human clinic.
Cafés, restaurants, pubs 6-monthly Airborne grease and cooking aerosols. This is genuinely the fastest-loading category we service.
Salons, barbers, beauty 6–12 monthly Aerosol product residue and keratin dust. Sooner if you spray a lot.
Shops, offices, dairies Annual Ordinary dust and foot traffic.
Vape stores More than annual Vegetable glycerine builds a sticky film on the coil that yearly cleaning does not keep up with.

These are starting intervals based on how fast each premises type loads, not a rule. After the first clean we will tell you what yours actually needs.

Common questions

What owners ask us first

If yours isn’t here, ring us. You will get an answer on the call rather than a form response two days later.

Will you be here during trading hours?

Only if you want us to be. Saturdays, your closed day, before you open or after you shut are all normal for us, and after hours is possible by arrangement. Tell us the window that costs you least and we will work inside it. We will not book a slot that suits our run and leave you to deal with it.

How long is each unit out of action?

Roughly 45 minutes per unit, although some units naturally require more time than others. For large premises we may send more than one technician. The unit is usable again as soon as we are finished with it — there is no drying-out period to wait through.

What do you do about mess, especially above a prep bench or a treatment chair?

Nothing gets touched until what is underneath it is covered. Drop sheets down, surfaces sheeted, and no water dripping down your wall. Then sheets up, surfaces wiped and our rubbish out with us.

Who pays — me or my landlord?

It depends on your lease, and we are not going to pretend to read it for you. Commercial leases split maintenance in all sorts of ways and we are heat pump cleaners, not your lawyer. What we can do is help you work out who to ask: tell us the building and the managing agent on the quote form and we will point you at the right person. Plenty of our commercial work is billed to a building manager rather than the tenant.

Do you do ducted and ceiling cassette units, or only high-wall?

All of them. Ceiling cassettes, ducted systems, floor consoles and high-wall units. Commercial fitouts are full of cassettes and ducted runs, and a lot of people assume a heat pump cleaner only does the box on the wall. We do not — and cassettes in particular are worth doing properly, because they sit directly above the room and drop straight down onto whatever is below.

What if the unit is not actually working properly?

A lot of the time that is what the clean fixes. Poor cooling, a smell on start-up and condensate overflow are usually loading problems, not failures. If it turns out to be a genuine fault, it goes on your service record and we refer it to a qualified refrigeration or electrical contractor and coordinate the visit. We will not quietly clean around a problem and leave you to find it in January.

Do you cover multiple sites?

Yes, and it is worth telling us up front — several sites planned together is a better price than the same sites quoted one at a time. Put the number of sites on the quote form and we will build it into one schedule with one invoice.
