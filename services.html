The Deep Clean

Our most popular service: A thorough clean of both the inside and outside units to keep your heat pump performing at its best.

  • Coil sanitisation and pressure washed
  • Performance and temperature testing
  • Additional units: each

Deep Clean Plus

Everything in the Deep Clean, plus a professionally applied PermaSafe mould prevention treatment for ongoing protection.

  • Antimicrobial surface protection applied
  • Helps prevent mould and microbial growth
  • Reduces odours and allergens
  • Supports healthier indoor air quality
Up to 1 hour

The Mould Master

Only deployed when significant mould growth is found inside the unit. We remove it at the source.

  • Full deep clean and inspection
  • Mould removed from inside the unit
  • PermaSafe applied to prevent recurrence
  • Musty odours at the source
Up to 2 hours

The Duct Devil

  • Full ducting inspection

  • Supply vents cleaned
1 hour
