The people behind it

We’re Ros & Max

A Christchurch family team who started AirCare because we saw locals being underserved. We wanted to do it better — and prove you don’t need a big corporate brand to deliver a top-shelf result.

Rosalind & Maxwell Founders of AirCare — Christchurch

Director · Customer Services · Co-Founder

Ros

A true Cantabrian, Ros studied Psychology and Sociology at Canterbury University before pursuing a career in public health as a pharmacy technician. A keen advocate for community oriented roles and service, Ros also spent a number of years supporting St Johns operations as an ambulance dispatch worker.

After watching Max grow in the refrigeration industry, a few honest conversations over a beer led to a shared goal: offer a genuinely better clean at a fairer price. AirCare is the result — independent, local, and built around real customer care.

Public health background Western riding Off-road motorbikes 1969 Vauxhall Viva
“We’re dedicated to building AirCare into a business which respects and values our customers — the best service in town at a price that makes sense.”
Managing Director · Lead Technician · Co-Founder

Maxwell

Max began his journey assisting technicians at large refrigeration firms, quickly progressing to installing and servicing heat pumps across Christchurch. He noticed something: commercial properties got plenty of attention, but domestic homes and rentals were often overlooked.

So he set out to change that. AirCare was founded on the belief that every home deserves the same level of care — done properly, by someone who takes genuine pride in their work.
